Five rooms, one floor

Shoot, develop, edit, print and show your work without leaving the building.

  • Photo studio

    Strobe, RGB and LED lighting, seamless backdrops, a styling corner and smart-lock access

    A full shooting setup for portraits, fashion, product and editorial work, with ceiling rigs, backdrops on rollers, a clothes rack and steamer, and an "On Air" sign so your session stays uninterrupted.

  • Darkroom

    Film developing and black-and-white printing with enlargers, safelights, tanks and proper ventilation.

  • Editing suite

    Fast desktops with Adobe apps, colour-calibrated monitors, Wacom tablets and card readers.

  • Print station

    Photo printing, flatbed scanning, paper cutting and binding. Print your work and publish it.

  • Gallery foyer

    Rotating exhibitions, pop-up markets and a workshop zone. Vendors can rent wall or shelf space to sell zines and prints.
