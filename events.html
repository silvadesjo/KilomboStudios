Workshop

Roots & Notes

Aromatherapy & Intentional Blending · In partnership with Concrete.Atelier

Our very first event at Kilombo Studios. Twelve participants gathered around the blending table to explore 17 curated essential oils, learn the art of top, middle, and base notes, and craft their own personal aromatherapy roller blend — guided by emotion, story, and instinct. Each participant went home with a custom blend and a Caverna Aroma diffuser from Concrete.Atelier.

🕐 2.5 hours
👥 12 participants
