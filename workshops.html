What we teach

Workshops we run regularly. Ask about private sessions for groups.

  • Intro to 35mm film photography

    Load your first roll, understand light and exposure, and leave with a roll ready to develop.

  • DIY film developing

    Develop your own negatives: the chemistry, the process, and how to scan and share the results.

  • Darkroom printing

    Make black-and-white prints from your own negatives and leave with three or four finished prints.

  • Photo zine making

    Fold, cut and bind your own zine from your images and handmade paper, over two sessions.

  • Batik printmaking

    Wax-resist dyeing on fabric, drawing on African textile traditions and contemporary print.

  • Paper making

    Turn shredded paper into handmade sheets for art, journals and zines.

  • Storytelling through photography

    Build a personal photo essay or series with editorial guidance.

  • Crochet and fibre arts

    Community skill-sharing: learn from people who carry craft knowledge, and share your own.

Roots & Notes

Aromatherapy and intentional blending, in partnership with Concrete.Atelier. Our first event at the studio.

Twelve participants gathered around the blending table to explore 17 essential oils, learn how top, middle and base notes work together, and craft a personal roller blend guided by emotion, story and instinct. Everyone went home with their blend and a Caverna Aroma diffuser from Concrete.Atelier.

Host a workshop

You don't need a teaching background, just a skill and the will to share it. Kilombo handles the rest.

  1. Tell us your idea

    Share the skill, the format and how many sessions you have in mind. No idea is too niche.

  2. We talk it through

    Joyce gets back to you within a week to confirm space, materials and a date.

  3. We handle the logistics

    Promotion, registration, space and admin are on us. You show up and teach.

  4. You get paid

    Facilitators receive a share of registration fees.

All disciplines are welcome: photography, craft, language, music, food, movement, technology, storytelling.
