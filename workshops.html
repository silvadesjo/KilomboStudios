Host a workshop

You don't need a teaching background, just a skill and the will to share it. Kilombo handles the rest.

  1. Tell us your idea

    Share the skill, the format and how many sessions you have in mind. No idea is too niche.

  2. We talk it through

    We get back to you within a week to confirm space, materials and a date.

  3. We handle the logistics

    Promotion, registration, space and admin are on us. You show up and teach.

  4. You get paid

    Facilitators receive a share of registration fees.

All disciplines are welcome: photography, craft, language, music, food, movement, technology, storytelling.
